# Funding and independence
All core functionality remains free without registration. Voluntary donations, institutional grants,
sponsors and separately contracted training/consulting can fund maintenance, documentation,
accessibility, reproducibility and measured infrastructure costs. No paywall, subscriptions or billing.

No donation account is verified; `.github/FUNDING.yml` is deliberately absent. GitHub Sponsors,
Ko-fi and other providers are candidates only. Before operational setup, verify current Serbia
recipient eligibility, payout bank/currency, identity checks, fees and lawful entity arrangements
against official provider guidance. Obtain Serbian accounting/tax advice appropriate to the actual
recipient and distinguish donations, grants and service revenue. No tax rate, exemption or payout
availability is assumed here. No payment service is activated by this project.

Publish quarterly receipts/cost categories and annual funding summaries without exposing donor
personal details. Disclose material sponsor conflicts. Sponsors cannot alter methods, suppress
findings, select favorable outcomes or veto corrections. Institutional modules follow the same
public review and license rules; disclose earmarked funding and retain maintainer independence.
Consulting contracts must not transfer control of public findings. Governance handles recusal and
recorded funding decisions. Add real verified links only after owner account verification.
