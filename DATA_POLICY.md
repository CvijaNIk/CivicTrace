# Data policy
Original project code, docs and synthetic fixtures are Apache-2.0. External data remains under
its source license, restrictions and privacy obligations. No real datasets are published yet.
Each source requires a manifest: publisher, URL, collection timestamp, source date/version,
hash, license/terms evidence, attribution, redistribution decision, transformations and exclusions.
Record terms as observed at acquisition and recheck before release. If rights are unclear,
provide only a connector specification and synthetic examples; hold redistribution.

Collect only needed fields. Exclude personal contact details and sensitive identifiers from public
examples. Public availability does not waive privacy obligations. Local financial and client data
must stay outside source control, CI logs, public issues, reports and public hosting. No telemetry
or automatic uploads. Do not submit audit, proprietary or whistleblower material to this project.

Source snapshots are immutable; corrections produce a new version with lineage and a changelog.
Report missing inputs and stale sources explicitly. Distinguish verified identity from fuzzy
candidates, reported facts from computations, and interpretation from allegations. Findings are
indicators, not proof of corruption, sanctions violations or crime. Follow governance for disputes.
